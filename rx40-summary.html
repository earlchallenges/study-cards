7 · Patient teaching

Low blood sugar rescue 🍬

Spot it. Shaky, sweaty, confused, or irritable.
Take 15 g of carbs. Glucose tablets or gel, hard candy, or 4 oz of juice or regular soda.
Keep a record. Write down each low and tell the provider.
Get help. Call the provider or 911 if it doesn't respond to treatment.

Low sugar

  • Shaky, nervous, sweaty, clammy
  • Irritable, confused
  • Blurry vision
  • Tingling lips, tongue, or cheeks
  • Headache, clumsiness
  • Nightmares, seizures

High sugar

  • High blood sugar
  • Sugar in the urine
  • Frequent urination
  • Increased thirst

Also teach them to

Wear medical ID. Check and record blood sugar. Be careful driving until they know how a medicine affects them. Check their feet every day. Never skip a dose because of cost: tell the provider instead.

Book p. 594–595. Newer guideline focus: continuous glucose monitors, personalized weight care, heart failure screening, bone health, and diabetes distress (p. 593).
